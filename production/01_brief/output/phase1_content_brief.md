# Layer 4: Briefing Report - Phase 1: Content Restructuring

## Feature: "Impact-First" Hero and Projects

### 1. Goals
- **Hiring Manager Focus:** Ensure "What Tharun does" and "What outcomes he has shipped" are clear in < 3 seconds.
- **Narrative Lead:** Move from a "list of skills" to a "story of impact."
- **Premium Identity:** Establish "Systems, Craft, and Performance" as the primary user impression.

### 2. Core Content Requirements
#### Hero Section
- **Status Line:** "Available for [Role/Project]" – signal availability clearly.
- **Dominant Headline:** High-conviction engineering identity.
- **Three Proof Stats:** (e.g., Performance, Projects Shipped, Years experience).
- **Two Actions:** (e.g., "See My Projects", "Get in Touch").

#### Project Section (Bento Layout)
- **Flagship Project:** One card must clearly dominate.
- **Three-Point Narrative:** Each project card must follow:
    1. **Problem:** What was the challenge?
    2. **Change Made:** What did you actually do?
    3. **Measurable Result:** What was the outcome?

### 3. Constraints
- **Preserve Sections:** Existing About, Skills, Experience sections must stay, but be ready for future restyling.
- **Recruiter Readable:** All "Impact" text must be concise and jargon-aware.
- **Mobile-First:** The content hierarchy must remain intact on small screens.
