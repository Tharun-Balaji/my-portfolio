# Problem Brief: Kinetic Terminal Redesign

## Problem
The existing portfolio communicates skill range, but its bright retro presentation no longer matches the darker, more systems-minded direction Tharun wants the site to project. The homepage needs to feel more intentional, technically credible, and visually cohesive without throwing away real portfolio content.

## Who is affected
Recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators who need to understand Tharun's strengths quickly while deciding whether to keep reading or reach out.

## Why it matters
The portfolio is the fastest way to communicate engineering taste. A stronger shell improves trust, makes the project archive feel more curated, and gives future case-study work a clearer home.

## Success criteria
- [ ] The homepage shifts from the current bright retro style into a dark kinetic terminal presentation.
- [ ] Existing portfolio data for projects, experience, and contact remains connected to the UI instead of being replaced with placeholder mock content.
- [ ] Navigation, responsive layout, and interactive surfaces still work on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] The redesign introduces clearer storytelling around hero positioning, stack framing, project selection, and writing or research direction.

## Non-goals
- Full CMS or admin tooling for the portfolio.
- A real AI assistant backed by an API.
- Inventing fake roles, publications, or credentials to match the reference wireframe.

## Open questions
- Which projects should become long-form case studies next?
- Should a resume file or downloadable PDF be added in a follow-up iteration?

## Effort and risk
| Dimension | Rating | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User impact | High | The change affects the full homepage experience and first impression. |
| Engineering effort | Medium | Mostly frontend restructuring and interaction work inside the Astro app. |
| Unknowns | Medium | The main risks are preserving performance and keeping the new direction grounded in real content. |
